Questions organisers ask
How early should a delegation engage you?
For Davos Week, 90 days or more. For other visits, 30 days is comfortable; we have delivered on far less.
Can you work alongside our own security detail?
Yes. We plan around your officers and the Swiss authorities, never instead of them.
Do you name past clients?
Only with written consent. Discretion is the default.
Which languages do your teams work in?
English, German, French, Italian and Hindi on the ground, more on request.

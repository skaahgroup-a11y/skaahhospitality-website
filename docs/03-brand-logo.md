# 03 · Brand Identity & Logo: SKAAH Hospitality

Files delivered in `/brand/logo/`. This identity derives from the SKAAH Group emblem (gold trade wheel with commodity icons around a globe) and translates it for hospitality: same DNA, quieter execution.

## 1. Design rationale
The Group emblem is inspired by the **Sudarshana Chakra held by Vishnu**: the discus of order, protection and rightful motion. The Hospitality mark keeps that lineage but expresses it as hospitality's oldest promise: **dawn over Switzerland**. A gold sun rises above white Alpine peaks and green foothills inside the Group's golden ring; its **eight floating rays carry the chakra's eightfold order**, engraved as fine strokes rather than a star, so the mark reads as welcome and arrival, never as heraldry. Two shimmer lines on the water complete the journey: the world arrives, the sun is up, Switzerland is ready. No black exists anywhere in the identity.

## 2. Palette and construction
| Colour | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep blue | `#164069` | Field disc, HOSPITALITY line (Vishnu blue, never black) |
| Gold pair | `#BFA05A` / `#9A7B2D` | Rings, sun and its eight engraved rays, water shimmer, SKAAH wordmark |
| Green pair | `#1E6E52` / `#175A43` | Alpine foothills |
| Light | `#F4F6F8` ice, `#D8E4F0` facet | Peak shading |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Peak faces |
Construction: blue disc in a double gold ring (wheel-rim heritage) · gold sun with eight round-capped stroke rays in a symmetric arc (chakra count preserved) · asymmetric twin peaks, white with an ice facet · rolling green foothills in two depths · two gold shimmer lines on the water beneath the sun · wordmark SKAAH in gold serif capitals, HOSPITALITY letterspaced in deep blue between gold rules. The emblem stays legible at 32 px (sun + peak silhouette), which makes the favicon honest.

## 4. Usage rules
- Clear space: height of the letter `S` on all sides. Minimum icon size 32 px; minimum lockup width 140 px.
- On photography: mono-white only, over the darkest third of the frame.
- Never: recolour, add shadows or gradients, place the colour emblem on mid-tone imagery, or pair with the Group's commodity icons.
- Website favicon: icon on blue `#164069`, exported 32/180/512 px (task T1.6).
- Print note: before any print use, convert wordmark text to outlines in a vector editor (listed as a follow-up task; web use is unaffected).

## Body outline (H2 sections, each 200-350 words, final copy drafted here)
1. **What WEF week really is on the ground.** Population multiplies, the valley has one road in, security perimeters reshape distances, and every scarce resource (beds, vehicles, kitchen capacity, staff) is contracted months out. The teams that succeed treat Davos as an operation, not a trip.
2. **The calendar that matters.** Copy gives the working countdown: `180 days: accommodation strategy and budget envelope · 120 days: blocks and vehicle contracts signed · 90 days: programme and protocol draft · 60 days: catering menus and staffing locked · 30 days: rehearsal of movements · Week 0: 24-hour operations rhythm.` (Rendered as a timeline component.)
3. **Accommodation reality.** Why delegations split between Davos, Klosters and the valley corridor; trade-offs of each; how master accounts and floor protocol work. No named hotels without commercial clearance `[TO CONFIRM]`.
4. **Movement and the one-road problem.** Convoy timing against security closures, shuttle discipline, rail as the underrated arrival, luggage logistics done separately from principals.
5. **Catering at altitude.** Feeding a delegation properly when kitchen capacity is the scarcest commodity in town; why we bring our own multi-cuisine capability including regional Indian, Jain and halal tables.
6. **Protocol in a compressed town.** Precedence when everyone is a principal; bilateral rooms; gifting; flags and titles; documentation rules.

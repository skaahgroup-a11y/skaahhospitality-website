# 02.00 · Content Guidelines (apply to every page)

## Voice
- Calm, confident, specific. Concrete promises, never superlatives. British English.
- Short sentences. Active voice. Second person for the reader ("your delegation"), first person plural for SKAAH ("we handle").
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Use commas, colons or parentheses.
- Numbers as figures (48 hours, 6 delegations, 100 MB). No exclamation marks.

## Claim rules (blocking)
1. Never mention "international organisations" or UN-type bodies.
2. Named states (Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Assam, Kerala) render only where the CMS field `consentGranted: true`. Default copy everywhere is the approved fallback: **"Six Indian state government delegations, four WEF years, one Swiss partner."**
3. Any statistic without a verified source is written as `[TO CONFIRM: description]` and must be resolved before launch.
4. Hero and section copy reads capability first, evidence second: "delegation capability, evidenced by India".
5. No security-sensitive detail ever: no routes, room numbers, plates, credentials, or images revealing them.

## Recurring copy blocks (single source, reused)
- **Response promise**: "Every enquiry receives a first response within 48 hours. Event and MICE RFPs within one business day."
- **Group line (footer)**: "A SKAAH Group company"
- **Descriptor**: "The Swiss hospitality and logistics partner for government delegations and high-value corporate groups, proven at WEF Davos."
- **Fallback proof line**: "Six Indian state government delegations. Four WEF years. One Swiss partner."
- **Contact block**: Opfikon (Zurich Airport), Switzerland · WhatsApp `[TO CONFIRM]` · Phone `[TO CONFIRM]` · enquiry@skaahhospitality.com `[TO CONFIRM]`

## Media brief format (used on every page)
Each image slot lists: `slot`, `subject`, `composition`, `source` (WEF-archive | new-shoot | licensed-stock-fallback), `alt` (literal description, no keyword stuffing), `treatment` (colour grade: cool alpine light, deep navy shadows, restrained gold accents; no HDR, no saturation push).
Film slots list: `duration`, `content`, `audio` (none at autoplay), `poster` (the LCP frame), `fallback` (poster only when reduced motion or Save-Data).

## Photography direction (global)
- People shown working, not posing: gloved hands, convoy marshals, kitchen pass, translation booth, luggage logistics at scale.
- Discretion first: faces of officials only with consent flag; otherwise backs, distance, silhouettes, motion blur.
- Swiss signature frames: Davos Promenade in snow, Zurich Airport apron at dawn, Lake Geneva mist, rail interiors, Alpine passes.
- Colour: navy and ice dominate, gold appears as light (lanterns, brass, dawn), never as graphic overlay on photography.

## SEO fields per page (defined in docs/06, values in each content file)
`metaTitle` (≤60 chars) · `metaDescription` (≤155) · `primaryKeyword` · `secondaryKeywords` · `ogImage` brief.
